# Product Strategy & OKRs: Fable Growth

> Module 1 · Write a Strategy That Can Survive a Test

## 1. Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Your choice |
|---|---|
| **Winning aspiration** | People open Fable when they are already stressed. Winning means they open Fable before the stress hits, because they know a hard moment is coming. |
| **Where to play** | Paying users aged 25 to 45 with a big life change coming up: a new job, a new baby, moving, exams, a wedding, the dark winter months. We start with people who already used Fable once during a hard time. We are not focusing on people who just want to relax every day, people who need a doctor or therapist, teenagers, or companies. |
| **How to win** | Users tell Fable what is coming up in their life. Fable makes a short plan that starts a few weeks before, using the meditations and sleep stories that helped them last time. Headspace and Calm wait for you to open the app. Fable reaches out before you need it. |
| **Capabilities required** | Our team must be good at four things. Making it easy for users to add what is coming up, like a calendar. Making short plans for the most common life events, using our existing meditations and sleep stories. Sending the right reminder at the right time before an event. Making sure the plans are safe and are not medical treatment. |
| **Management systems** | We count how many paying users add at least one upcoming event, how many finish their plan before the event, and how many are still paying six months later. The team looks at these numbers every month, and we show them to the board every three months. |

## 2. Your one hard no

> **Hard no:** We will not keep making more general content, like new meditations and sleep stories for everyone. We only make plans for specific life events. More of the same content does not give people a reason to come back.

## 3. OKR cascade

> **Objective:** Make Fable the app people use to get ready for life's big moments.
>
> - **KR1:** 3 in 10 paying users add at least one upcoming life event in their first three months.
> - **KR2:** 6 in 10 users who add an event finish their plan before the event.
> - **KR3:** The share of paying users still paying after six months goes from about 30% to 45%.
